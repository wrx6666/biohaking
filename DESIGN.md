## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural and sensorial balance between state-of-the-art biohacking precision and nurturing family medical care. The brand personality conveys longevity, metabolic vitality, clinical authority, and enduring warmth. 

The aesthetic is **Clinical Precision infused with Warm Organic Tactility**:
- Crisp, clinical micro-grid structures and technical charting textures signal rigorous diagnostic technology and scientific longevity.
- Soft, milky ivory base planes and muted botanical sage tones counterbalance technical sharpness with regenerative tranquility.
- Fluid, refractive glassmorphic overlays suggest clarity, cell membrane permeability, and pristine hygiene.
- Orbital vector motifs and constellation lineages evoke multi-generational health tracking and holistic bodily systems.
- Every viewport projects deep atmospheric calm, removing the cold anxiety of legacy healthcare while avoiding the superficiality of wellness fads.

## Colors

The palette grounds the interface in regenerative natural science and diagnostic lucidity:

- **Primary (`#2d5a43` - Deep Sage):** The foundation of medical trust and cellular renewal. Used for principal navigation accents, primary callouts, verified diagnostic stamps, and deep contrast anchors.
- **Secondary (`#a4d65e` - Bio-Lime Accent):** A high-vitality catalyst denoting active metabolic markers, biohacking optimization scores, telemetry pulses, and interactive focal points. Deployed predominantly as tinted washes (10–25% opacity) and precise telemetry indicators to preserve clinical sophistication.
- **Tertiary (`#dce5dd` - Clinical Mist):** A balanced, mineral-toned gray-green used for hairline technical grids, divider boundaries, structural frames, and inactive sensor tracks.
- **Neutral (`#1a2621` - Charcoal Forest):** A dense, bio-organic graphite replacing pure black for ultra-legible typography, technical labels, and high-contrast structural data.

### Extended Environmental Tiers
- **Canvas Base:** Milk White / Warm Ivory (`#fafbf8`) serving as the primary unpigmented backdrop.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure Alabaster (`#ffffff`) with subtle jade-tinted borders.
- **Surface Substrates:** Sage Wash (`#eaf2eb`) and Pale Dew (`#f2f7f2`) for table striping, nested biomarker groups, and card recessed wells.
- **Micro-Accents:** Lime Mist (`rgba(198, 232, 155, 0.18)`) for biological trend halos and data hover envelopes.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances medical humanities with scientific calibration:

- **Editorial & Humanities (Newsreader):** Brings warmth, prestige, and longevity wisdom to primary headings, doctor profiles, philosophical anti-age tenets, and health diagnostics overviews. It evokes timeless clinical mastery without feeling detached.
- **Structural Interface (Manrope):** Delivers clean geometry, exceptional legibility at all densities, and friendly open apertures for family clinical records, operational text, action menus, and long-form consultation summaries.
- **Precision Telemetry (JetBrains Mono):** Governs physiological readouts, genomic sequencers, dosage specs, chronological timestamps, and lab chart watermarks. It guarantees monospaced tabular alignment for biological data streams.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column adaptive fluid grid structured by an underlying 8px modular baseline with a sub-tier 4px micro-grid.

### Background Rhythm & Environmental Layering
- **Micro-Grid Underlay:** A permanent, ultra-subtle vector mesh (`#dce5dd` rendered at 12–18% opacity) runs in 32px increments across the background, evoking graphing paper and clinical charts.
- **Orbital Vector Watermarks:** Multi-ring astronomical and cellular orbit vectors subtly intersect sections at scale, designating family lineage relationships and holistic systemic feedback loops.
- **Section Margins:** Generous vertical spacing (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) provides clinical breathing room, reducing cognitive strain during dense biohacking evaluation reviews.

### Breakpoints & Adaptation
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, compact gutters (`gutter-mobile`), single-column diagnostic feeds, sticky simplified health telemetry ribbon.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout, intermediate gutters, side-by-side comparative biomarker tracking.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column system, max container width of 1440px centered, multi-layered telemetry panels alongside narrative longevity dossiers.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through **Liquid Glass Refraction and Tonal Substrates**, deliberately avoiding thick muddy drop shadows in favor of luminous clinical translucency.

- **Surface Levels:**
  - *Base Substrate:* `#fafbf8` matte backdrop carrying vector micro-grids and organic contour watermarks.
  - *Mid-Plane Panels:* `#ffffff` with 1px border of `#dce5dd` at 80% opacity, accompanied by an ambient, diffused contact shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(45, 90, 67, 0.04)`.
  - *Elevated Diagnostics & Modals:* Liquid glass membranes using `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(140%)`, edged with a crisp dual border: `inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` and `outer 0 0 0 1px rgba(220, 229, 221, 0.6)`.
- **Bio-Luminescent Glows:** For optimal biomarker scores and live longevity telemetry, employ soft localized halos: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(164, 214, 94, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The geometric approach balances organic cellular curves with clinical structural confidence. 

- **Border Radii:** The primary roundedness tier is set to `2` (`0.5rem` / `8px` for buttons, input forms, and status chips; `1rem` / `16px` for structural cards and biomarker pods; `1.5rem` / `24px` for high-level editorial showcases and liquid modal frames).
- **Cellular Geometry:** Select diagnostic markers and visual avatar rings use true organic circular bounds (full pill/circle), referencing blood cells, biological membranes, and planetary family constellations.
- **Vector Strokes:** Structural icons and line diagrams utilize precise 1.25px to 1.5px vector weights with slightly softened terminal caps to reinforce surgical tactility.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Bio-Action:** Deep Sage (`#2d5a43`) background, `#fafbf8` text, `0.5rem` border radius. Subtle hover shift to `#244936` with a faint `rgba(164, 214, 94, 0.2)` edge reflection.
- **Secondary (Cellular Glass):** Semi-transparent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), 1px `#dce5dd` border, `#2d3b34` text, soft blur on backdrop.
- **Telemetry Action (Optimization / Biohack):** Pale Lime tint (`rgba(164, 214, 94, 0.15)`) with a vibrant `#2d5a43` label and a 1px border in `#c6e89b`.

### Cards & Diagnostic Panels
- **Biomarker Data Card:** White background layered over the micro-grid canvas. Top-right corner displays monospaced sequence code (e.g., `BIO-094-VTL`). Left edge carries an optional 3px vertical status indicator (Sage for optimal, Soft Lime for active therapy, Clinical Gray for baseline).
- **Family Constellation Card:** Interlinked orbital paths framing interconnected patient profiles (e.g., child, parent, elder) with translucent node badges and age-bracket biomarker averages.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Fields:** Recessed `#fafbf8` surfaces with a 1px `#dce5dd` resting border. On focus, transition smoothly to a 1px `#2d5a43` border accompanied by an ambient `0 0 0 3px rgba(164, 214, 94, 0.2)` focus ring. Placeholder text rendered in muted Charcoal Forest (`#4a5c53`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved squircle checkboxes and circular radio wells with a 1.5px `#2d5a43` stroke. Checked state fills with `#2d5a43` displaying a crisp white micro-tick or lime active core.

### Chips & Telemetry Tags
- **Status Chips:** Height of 26px, typography in JetBrains Mono (`label-technical`). 
  - *Optimal:* Background `#eaf2eb`, text `#2d5a43`, dot indicator `#a4d65e`.
  - *Monitoring:* Background `#f4f6f1`, text `#4a5c53`, dot indicator `#dce5dd`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Laboratory Chart Watermark:** High-precision SVG background elements depicting stylized DNA helix ribbons, metabolic cycle flowcharts, or subtle cardiac vector traces embedded inside complex data panels at 4% to 8% opacity.
- **Longevity Trajectory Bar:** Horizontal dual-track bar with a deep sage completed baseline, an animated pulsing Lime marker (`#a4d65e`), and clinical numerical delta annotations pinned above.
